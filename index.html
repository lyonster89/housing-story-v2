Is housing supply keeping up with household growth?

This visualization compares household formation, new housing, mortgage rates, household income, and the cost of financing a $200,000 home.

Begin the story

Scene 1

Is the housing market keeping up?

New households represent added demand. Housing completions represent newly completed homes entering the housing stock. Comparing the two shows whether new construction kept pace with household growth.

Annual housing gap New housing completions − New households

A positive gap means construction exceeded household growth. A negative gap means household growth exceeded construction.

Important interpretation notes

Housing completions are used as the measure of new housing available. Mortgage payments include principal and interest only. The comparison is national, so it does not capture local shortages or differences among metropolitan areas.

Data sources include the Federal Reserve Economic Data service, U.S. Census Bureau, and Zillow.
